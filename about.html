About Us

The University of Chicago Science Olympiad is an undergraduate Registered Student Organization founded in 2017 with the mission to provide a rigorous Science Olympiad experience for middle and high school students in the Chicagoland area and across Illinois. In addition to hosting our tournaments during the winter quarter, we also work in the wider Illinois Science Olympiad community as test writers and event supervisors and engage with the Chicago Public Schools and South side schools to expand access to STEM programs such as Science Olympiad. UCSO is entirely run by UChicago undergraduates and alumni, and our Division B and C invitationals would not be possible without the tireless dedication and effort of our board and members.

Alumni

The UCSO Alumni Network not only inspires our current members to continue trailblazing in the sciences, but has also made this on campus chapter a pinnacle of our civic engagement experience. We’d like to honor our previous presidents, board members, mentors, and leaders who taught us the power of Science Olympiad and inspired generations of youth to pursue higher education in STEM.
