Community Impact

The University of Chicago Science Olympiad is committed to increasing STEM opportunities in Chicago. This year, we are working with UChicago Quantum Society and the National Science Olympiad to develop a quantum-focused event. We also hosted a table at the 2025 South Side Science Festival. We are also partnering with the Illinois Science Olympiad to host a South Side Science Olympiad partial tournament day for schools on Chicago's South side. Finally, we are excited to launch a mentorship opportunity to Chicago Public Schools schools looking to start a team or build their existing team.
